01 · Foundation

Your role

Tadawa has Family Medicine specialists only on the platform. You handle Treat Me Now calls, remote appointments, care plan requests and in-clinic consultations with the Tafileh team.

You are the gatekeeper

You decide whether a referral to a specialist or the ER is needed, and you make the referral on the insurer portal.

On-site General Practitioners

Triage patients in Tafileh and act as your assistant doctors. They do not open insurance forms or order labs, imaging or medication.

Family Doctors only

Open forms on insurer portals (including GP-type accounts), order tests and prescribe.

You also work with the nurses and secretaries in Tafileh. When we refer a patient to a specialty, the insurer opens access to all in-network specialists of that type and the patient chooses the doctor.

03 · Calls

Status and call routing

Off shift means logged out

When off shift, log out completely. "Away" or "Not available" is not enough. Opening Tadawa while off shift, even on your phone to check a patient file, can route Treat Me Now calls to you; log out immediately afterwards.

  • The secretary may contact you on phone while you are off shift to follow up on a patient you saw.
  • Keep Tadawa open in only one browser tab.
  • Patients cannot choose a doctor; the system routes the call to an available doctor. If a patient asks for a specific doctor, the other doctors set themselves to "Not available" until the call connects, then switch back to "Available".
  • The status at the top of the screen is the one that counts. If it conflicts with your profile, or callers get "busy" while you are Available, switch to Unavailable and back to Available.
  • If the doctor who receives a call does not answer, the call is not passed on. The patient has to call again, and the next attempt goes to an available doctor.
  • If a call rings while you are marked Not available or Away, report it with a screenshot in #IT-Support.
Callback Queue
  1. If all Family Doctors are busy, the patient is told no doctor is available.
  2. All eligible Family Doctors and the clinic secretary get a missed-call notification, and a consultation with the status "Missed Call" appears in the appointments table and on the dashboard.
  3. Click "Call Back Patient" to call the patient (it can take about a minute to open).
  4. If the call-back does not connect, ask the clinic to call the patient by phone.
Call priority

Emergencies always come first. If you are busy and another patient's call is missed, the secretary calls that patient to say you will call back as soon as you finish.

04 · Clinical

Running a telemedicine consultation

Summary of the Tadawa Telemedicine Doctor Handbook. Read the full handbook once; use this section as a quick reference.

The Tadawa Golden Rule

  1. Ask
  2. Observe
  3. Assess
  4. Decide
  5. Explain
  6. Safety-net
  7. Document
Before the call
  • Review the record: previous consultations, diagnoses, medications, allergies and results.
  • Check your camera, microphone and internet.
  • Professional presence: professional dress (working from home does not change this), a plain or office background, front lighting, camera at eye level, a quiet private room, no food, no personal calls and nobody else in view.
Opening
"Hello, I'm Dr. ___, one of the family physicians at Tadawa. Can you hear and see me clearly?"
  • Confirm full name, date of birth and identity. For children, confirm the guardian's name and relationship.
  • Ask where the patient is (essential if they may be unstable: exact location, who is with them, access to emergency services).
  • Confirm privacy and who else is present. Start with an open question: "How can I help you today?"
History, vitals and examination
  • Take a structured history: presenting complaint, relevant history, all medications and supplements, allergies (drug, reaction, severity).
  • Actively screen for red flags (chest pain, headache, abdominal pain, breathing). Red flags trigger a decision, not more questions.
  • Ask for available measurements (temperature, BP, heart rate, SpO₂, glucose, weight) and when they were taken. Ask to see the device on camera if important.
  • Remote examination is limited. Do not use self-palpation to rule out an acute abdomen. Do not document a normal ear drum unless it was actually seen. Do not make a confident diagnosis from poor images.
  • Children: confirm weight; observe alertness, feeding, breathing and hydration. Give guardians simple instructions; never ask them to "perform an examination".
Decide

Can I safely manage this patient remotely?

Yes

Continue.

Maybe / uncertain

Arrange an in-person assessment.

No / possible emergency

Urgent or emergency referral.

Before prescribing, check diagnosis, allergies, current medications, interactions, contraindications, pregnancy, renal/hepatic issues, dose, frequency, duration and follow-up. Do not prescribe only because the patient asks.

Close
  • Explain the likely diagnosis, the treatment, what to expect and what to do if there is no improvement.
  • Safety-netting is mandatory and specific to the problem.
  • Never end with "follow up as needed" alone when follow-up matters: say when, where and who is responsible.
  • Confirm understanding (ask the patient to repeat how they will take the medication and which symptoms need urgent care).
Document
  • Date/time, modality, identity, participants, location if relevant, consent.
  • Complaint, history, medications, allergies, vitals, remote examination, assessment and plan (treatment, investigations, referral, follow-up, safety-netting).
  • Write "Physical examination was limited by the telemedicine format", then document what you actually observed.

Emergency suspected

Stop the routine consultation

"I am concerned about your symptoms and I don't think it is safe to manage this through telemedicine alone."

  1. Establish the patient's location and who is with them, and assess immediate safety.
  2. Direct the patient to emergency care, make the referral (Section 10) and report in the #Tafileh Clinic 24/7 group.
  3. Document the recommendation, whether the patient accepted, and any contact with the receiving facility.
Never

Pretend an examination was done; give false reassurance; continue video with an unstable patient; delay an emergency referral; prescribe antibiotics just because they are requested; end without a safety net.

05 · Calls

Treat Me Now troubleshooting

Patient does not join

Ask the secretary or nurse in the #Tafileh Clinic 24/7 group to call the patient. The patient can rejoin the same call with the reconnect button. Wait up to 10 minutes. If the patient still has not joined, cancel the consultation so the patient is not charged.

Patient drops during the call

Wait 5 minutes for the patient to call back, then save the consultation.

No sound
  • Patient side: update the Tadawa app, allow the microphone, turn off Bluetooth and use speakerphone.
  • Your side: allow the microphone and camera in the browser, test at mictests.com, close other apps and Tadawa tabs, then refresh or restart.
  • Still no sound: continue on Zoho Cliq, or by phone through the clinic, or let the secretary connect you with the patient.
After every call

Click "Save consultation". If you do not save, you stay "Busy" and will not receive new calls. If your status looks wrong or calls do not arrive: switch to Unavailable and back to Available, refresh, and keep Tadawa in one tab only.

Tadawa is down

See patients on Zoho Cliq and record the visits on Tadawa once it is back. Report it in #IT-Support with a screenshot.

06 · Schedule

Appointments, care plans and orders

  • Today's appointments are on the dashboard; later ones are in the Appointments section. Search a patient's name in Appointments to see previous visits.
  • Telemedicine appointments: click "Start consultation" at the appointment time. The patient cannot join early. The appointment stays open about 2 hours, but the system waits only about 15 minutes for the patient.
  • Care plan requests: open the request. If the patient gave enough information, create the care plan. If not, the secretary books a remote appointment with you.
  • Appointments made by the Irbid clinic may appear on our dashboard; check with the Irbid group before cancelling.
Orders

Always add lab and imaging orders in the orders section on Tadawa; otherwise the patient cannot see them. Patients can upload results in the app or send them to the clinic WhatsApp.

07 · Records

Documentation rules on Tadawa

Public notes

Visible to the patient and all treating doctors.

Private notes

Seen only by the doctor who wrote them.

  • The patient should call from their own (sub-account) profile so the claim and notes are recorded under the right person.
  • A parent calls from their own account for a child: document the consultation under the child, remove diagnoses and medications from the parent's record, and keep the parent's note with a disclaimer that the call was for the child.
  • Drug not in Tadawa's medication list: write it in the note and prescribe it on the insurer portal. Send missing drug names to the IT team in #IT-Support. Report wrong dosage forms (e.g., tablet listed as liquid) through the reporting form (Section 13).

08 · Prescribing

Medications, sick leaves and reports

Chronic medications

A formal medical report is required for chronic medications, including dose changes or a new chronic medication. The patient submits it to the insurer for coverage approval.

  • Before writing it: confirm the patient is taking the medication, check the date of the last follow-up labs, and order labs if needed.

Wording

"Patient [name, age, date] was seen at our Tafileh centre and suffers from [condition]. He/she requires [drug, dose] as a chronic medication."
Sign with your name and "Family Medicine Specialist".

The template (تقرير طبي للأدوية المزمنة) is available from the secretariat.

From your text to the patient's PDF
  1. Send the report text to the secretary on Zoho Cliq in the #Chronic Reports channel.
  2. The secretary puts it on the Tadawa Healthcare letterhead, prints, stamps and scans it.
  3. The secretary sends the scanned copy to the patient through WhatsApp.
  4. The secretary posts the same copy back in #Chronic Reports for documentation.

Unavailable medication and editing forms

  • The patient brings the name of an alternative available in Tafileh pharmacies. Remove the old drug and add the alternative on the form; the clinic informs the patient.
  • Any Family Doctor can edit a patient's form on the MedNet portal (dose, quantity, adding or replacing a drug). You do not need the doctor who wrote the original prescription.
  • Items already dispensed by the pharmacy cannot be edited.

Injections

  • Injections given from clinic stock must still be prescribed on the insurance form, so the patient dispenses them and returns them to the clinic.
  • Add an "IM injection" procedure; the administration fee covers syringes.

Sick leaves and reports

  • You decide sick leaves and document the duration in the patient's Public Note, then tell the Tafileh secretariat, who issue and stamp the official form and send it on WhatsApp. Only your name is required; you do not need your personal stamp, as the secretariat stamps the form with the Tadawa stamp.
  • If a GP writes a medical report for a patient you saw, the GP will ask for your approval.
  • Eye reports and glasses prescriptions are issued by Samara Centre, not Tadawa.

09 · Insurance

Insurance rules

Accepted insurers and TPAs

  • MedNet
  • Medexa
  • Newton
  • MedService – Company
  • MedService – Phosphate
  • Omni Care
  • Solidarity
  • GIG
  • Arab European
  • Bar Association

MedService Company and MedService Phosphate are different insurers. Logins and portal steps: see the restricted credentials document.

Pricing

  • Enter every procedure price manually from the Jordanian Medical Association fee schedule. A blank price causes rejection or partial approval.
  • Price = points × point value (2.8 JOD minimum to 3.5 JOD maximum). Family Medicine and GP prices differ.
  • Dressings (MedNet): simple 3 JOD, large 8 JOD, maximum 5 per form.
  • If an in-clinic procedure is rejected, post the patient's name and insurance number in the #Tafileh Clinic 24/7 group; the responsible person contacts the insurer.
Example

Family Doctor ECG: 9.6 points = 26.88–33.6 JOD.

When a form is used

No form used

Consultations with no medication, lab/imaging request or opened claim. Referring right after the history, with no tests.

Form used

If tests are ordered, a form is used even if you refer later.

Referral forms never use one of the patient's forms; the receiving facility opens its own.

Follow-ups and merging

  • If the patient returns within 7 days for the same complaint, edit the original form instead of opening a new one.
  • If several forms were opened by mistake, post in the #Tafileh Clinic 24/7 group: patient name, national ID, insurance ID and "دمج نماذج". Merging happens on working days (Sunday–Thursday). Never merge a referral form with a form that has diagnoses or labs.
  • Before saying visits are finished, check for a visit in the last 7 days and add orders to that form. If all visits are used, the secretary asks the patient to contact their insurer; follow up once new forms are added.

Coverage notes

  • Corns and calluses are often treated as cosmetic; call approvals with the clinical justification.
  • Crepe and compression bandages are usually not covered or only partly.
  • Some plans do not cover 100%, which can lead to rejected tests.
If labs are rejected

Use symptoms as the diagnosis, add a justification with the "+" next to the diagnosis, or call approvals. Only Family Doctors open forms. Use the Family Doctor account for Family Medicine orders; a Family-specialist order opened on a GP-type account is rejected.

10 · Referrals

Gatekeeping and referrals

Tamweelcom

Tadawa is the exclusive, mandatory primary care provider for Tamweelcom employees (1,476 beneficiaries). All other providers are closed to them; they need a Tadawa referral.

  • Emergencies: Tamweelcom patients contact Tadawa first (app or phone). You make the ER referral on MedNet.
  • Urgent referral requests from Tamweelcom HR (posted by management) are handled immediately by the Family Doctor on shift, even without a Treat Me Now call, and confirmed in the #Tafileh Clinic 24/7 group.
MedNet referral (Family Doctor)
  1. Open the patient's page on MedNet with the national ID or card number and date of birth.
  2. On the left, choose "referring specialty".
  3. ER referrals: select "general". Do NOT select "Emergency Medicine" or "ER" (the ER will not see it).
  4. Specialty referrals: select the required specialty.
  5. Diagnosis on all referrals: referral of pt w/o examination or treatment, even if you made a diagnosis. This stops a form being deducted.
  6. Leave "requested amount" blank and click Submit.
  • Do not call approvals for routine referrals. Ignore hospital staff who say it should be "ER".
  • If the hospital cannot find the referral, delete the form and create a new one with the same details; if it still fails, call MedNet approvals.
  • If MedNet auto-fills the form or "general" is missing (usually already referred that month), do not refer on that form. Send a screenshot to MedNet approvals and ask them to approve it (through the secretary).
  • "Visited a specialty without a referral" message: check whether we referred the patient. If not, refer now; if yes, the secretary calls MedNet approvals and informs the patient.
Document the referral on Tadawa too
  1. For every Treat Me Now call or appointment where you refer, open "Referrals" on Tadawa.
  2. Select the specialty. For ER referrals choose "ER / Emergency Medicine" (unlike MedNet).
  3. Set the "date From – To" fields and click Save.

Referrals for patients who call by phone are made directly on MedNet, then documented on Tadawa: create an appointment for the patient on Tadawa, then refer the patient on the platform.

Referral reporting on Zoho Cliq

Whenever a patient is referred, send a message in the #Tafileh Clinic 24/7 group confirming that the referral has been made, with the patient's information and the referral destination.

  • Patient name
  • Insurance number
  • Referral to (specialty / department)
  • Year of birth

Emergency referral

The secretary follows up with the patient an hour after the referral.

Specialist referral

The secretary follows up the next day.

After the follow-up call, the secretary confirms in the same group that the patient was followed up.

Radiology in Tafileh

MedNet does not cover CT or MRI at government or military hospitals in Tafileh, and there are no other CT/MRI centres there. In emergencies the public hospital receives the patient, who signs a financial waiver. Otherwise refer to MedNet-approved imaging centres in Karak (ask in the #Tafileh Clinic 24/7 group for the current list).

11 · Safety

Psychiatric emergencies and suicide risk

Follow the internal protocol. It applies to any patient with suicidal thoughts, intent or a recent attempt; acute psychosis endangering self or others; severe agitation or aggression; or any acute disturbance that stops them deciding safely.

Immediate safety

The patient is never left alone, is moved to a safe place, and harmful objects are removed (nursing does this in clinic).

Assessment: vital signs, consciousness, rule out medical causes (hypoglycaemia, hypoxia, intoxication, head injury), mental state, and suicide risk (thoughts, plan, means, intent, previous attempts, risk and protective factors).

High riskPlan, means, recent attempt, command hallucinations, safety cannot be ensured → call an ambulance/Civil Defence or the security authorities and transfer immediately to the ER or a hospital with psychiatry.
Moderate riskThoughts without a clear plan, several risk factors → consult a psychiatrist if possible; urgent referral to the ER or psychiatric clinic.
Low riskPassing thoughts, no plan or intent, strong protective factors → safety plan, early follow-up, psychiatry referral, and involve the family with consent (or without it if needed to protect life).

Document: times, complaint, findings, risk assessment, mental state, decision and reasons, people contacted, ambulance call time, departure time, receiving facility, and any refusal with the patient's capacity and the steps you took.

13 · Reporting

Reporting

One form for all reporting

All reporting goes through one form, the reporting form: نموذج الإبلاغ عن حادث أو مشكلة. Use the form every time, even if you already mentioned the case in a Zoho Cliq group or by phone. A message in a group is not a report.

Report these through the form

  • Any incident, bug or error, with a screenshot or video.
  • Problems with the Tadawa platform that patients or staff face, with the details and the action taken. Also post platform problems in #IT-Support.
  • Wrong dosage forms in Tadawa's medication list (e.g., a tablet listed as a liquid).
  • Resistant patients: patients who refuse to comply with medical instructions or procedures.
  • Abusive patients: patients who behave abusively or inappropriately.
  • Rare or unique medical services you provided.
Why it matters

Every report lands in one place, so each case is followed up and nothing gets lost in chat messages. It gives Tadawa a documented record in line with approved procedures, which protects patients and staff. Repeated problems become visible, so they can be fixed.
